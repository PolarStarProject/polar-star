# San Luis Regional Detention Center: share held more than a year well above other facilities

Open since October 9, 2026. From Polar Star Project, a public-interest data page; the figures are counts from public records, never about a person.

## What the records show

- 43 of the 109 people held here on August 6, 2026 had been in ICE detention more than a year (39%), against 6.3% of everyone held. At the national share, a count this high would come up by chance less than once in 100. Long stays often come from cases still in court or on appeal; the records do not say which.
