# Adelanto ICE Processing Center: deaths well above other facilities

Open since October 9, 2026. From Polar Star Project, a public-interest data page; the figures are counts from public records, never about a person.

## What the records show

- 4 deaths ICE reported here from August 2025 to July 2026: 24.4 for every 10,000 people held for a year, against 6.2 across all facilities on ICE's list over the same months. At the overall rate, a count this high would come up by chance about 2 times in 100.
- Officials were refused entry here 1 time since August 2025. This is listed beside the figures above as a fact; it does not show that one caused the other.
- About 1,635 people a day were held here from August 2025 to July 2026. Rates from a handful of deaths move a lot with one event.
