# South Texas ICE Processing Center: sexual abuse allegations well above other facilities

Open since October 9, 2026. From Polar Star Project, a public-interest data page; the figures are counts from public records, never about a person.

## What the records show

- ICE's audit of January 9, 2024 counted 43 sexual abuse allegations (reports made, not findings that abuse happened): 31.5 for every 1,000 people held for a year, against 9.4 across audited facilities. At the overall rate, a count this high would come up by chance less than once in 100.
- About 1,363 people a day were held here over the audit's period.
