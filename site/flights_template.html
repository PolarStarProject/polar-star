Drag to turn the globe. Lines are the positions the aircraft actually broadcast; a gap or a straight jump means the signal was lost.

    How flights are picked. Only aircraft flying under a contractor callsign are read. A day is kept when the aircraft starts or ends a flight at Alexandria, Harlingen, or Mesa, the airports ICE uses for staging. Contractor planes also fly ordinary charters, and flights that avoid those three airports are missed, so this is a minimum, not a total. Nobody on board is identified.

    "Last seen near" means the signal was lost before landing; the airport named is the closest one, not a confirmed landing.
